൧The studio

The studio

Praana means breath — the quiet force that moves through everything. It is also a small rooftop studio in Fort Kochi: a red floor, a painted sun, and a practice shared as a way of life, not just something you do on the mat.

Praana welcomes every level, from the first sun salutation to the first freestanding handstand. Classes are intuitive yet structured, rooted in traditional Hatha, and taught with the same care whether you are passing through Kochi for a morning or staying for a season.

൫The teacher

Nithin Xavier

  • Intuitiveyet structured
  • Firmyet compassionate

Nithin adapts his approach to each student’s body, background and intention, which is why every class feels personal, grounded and uplifting.

His work has taken him across borders, including workshops and private sessions in Japan. Yet whether teaching abroad or on the rooftop in Kochi, he brings the same depth of care, curiosity and connection.

What sets him apart is not just skill, but discipline, humility and a sincere dedication to sharing yoga as a way of life.

൬Philosophy

Philosophy: breath, balance and practice

  1. പ്രാണ

    PrāṇaBreath, the vital force

    Every class begins and ends with the breath. Movement follows it, and the mind learns to rest on it.

  2. സമത്വം

    SamatvamEvenness, balance

    Yogaḥ samatvam asti: yoga is balance. Effort and ease, strength and softness, on the mat and off it.

  3. അഭ്യാസം

    AbhyāsaSteady practice

    Discipline, humility and patience. Returning to the mat, day after day, until practice becomes a way of life.
