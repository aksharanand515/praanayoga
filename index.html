Welcome to Praana

Breathe. Move. Balance.

Praana means breath, the quiet force that moves through everything. Ours is a small rooftop studio in Fort Kochi with a red floor and a painted sun, where yoga is shared as a way of life, not just something you do on the mat.

Whether you are taking your very first sun salutation or working towards your first freestanding handstand, passing through Kochi for a morning or staying for a season, there is a space here for you to grow.

About the studio

A little studio with a big heart

Praana is a rooftop shala above Mackenzie Lane in Fort Kochi. Classes are intuitive yet structured, rooted in traditional Hatha and taught with the same care for complete beginners and seasoned practitioners alike.

Each session adapts to the bodies in the room. Expect patient, hands-on guidance, a good focus on strength and alignment, and a practice that leaves you calmer than you arrived.

Your teacher

Nithin Xavier

Lead teacher, Praana · Co-founder, Samya Retreats

Nithin’s teaching is intuitive yet structured, firm yet compassionate. He adapts his approach to each student’s body, background and intention, so every class feels personal, grounded and uplifting.

His work has taken him across borders, including workshops and private sessions in Japan. Whether teaching abroad or on the rooftop in Kochi, he brings the same depth of care, curiosity and connection.

Our philosophy

Yogah samatvam asti

Yoga is balance. Three ideas sit underneath every class.

  1. Prāṇa Breath

    പ്രാണ

    Every class begins and ends with the breath. Movement follows it, and the mind learns to rest on it.

  2. Samatvam Balance

    സമത്വം

    Effort and ease, strength and softness, on the mat and off it.

  3. Abhyāsa Practice

    അഭ്യാസം

    Discipline, humility and patience: returning to the mat, day after day, until practice becomes a way of life.
