Photography, filming & social media

We record our classes for social media. Nithin and the Praana team regularly photograph and film classes, workshops, private sessions, retreats and the teacher training on the rooftop and elsewhere. We share these photos and videos on Instagram (@praanayoga), on other social media, and on this website.

By joining a class, you agree that you may appear in these photos and videos, and that Praana may use them to share and promote the studio, without payment.

  • Prefer not to be filmed? Just tell your teacher before class begins. We will keep you out of frame, or we will not publish footage in which you can be recognised.
  • Changed your mind later? Write to us on WhatsApp or by email and we will take down any post in which you appear.
  • Children under 18 are only photographed or filmed with a parent’s or guardian’s permission.

Classes and bookings

Daily classes are held at 07:30 and 16:00 and last 75 minutes. Please arrive ten minutes early, or thirty minutes early for morning pranayama. Class times can change for holidays and special events; we will let you know when you book.

Private sessions and workshops are by appointment and subject to availability. If you cannot make a session you have booked, please tell us as early as you can, so we can offer the time to someone else.

Your health

Yoga is physical. Before class, tell your teacher about any injury, illness, pregnancy or condition that could affect your practice, and always practise within your own limits. You take part at your own risk, and if you are unsure whether yoga is right for you, please speak to a doctor first.

Our teachers give gentle hands-on adjustments. Tell your teacher at any time if you would rather not be adjusted, and we will respect that.
